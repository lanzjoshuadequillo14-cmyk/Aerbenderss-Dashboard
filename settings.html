Automation Thresholds

Define the environmental conditions that automatically activate the connected AeroPlug devices. These thresholds are read by the ESP32 firmware to determine when to trigger the relays.

Hysteresis Notice: An activated outlet will deactivate when the air-quality reading falls more than 15% below its configured threshold.

Audible Alert (Buzzer)

AeroCube Local Buzzer
--

Controls the AeroCube's local buzzer alert. When ON, the buzzer will sound during poor air quality conditions. When SILENCED, the buzzer is muted but air quality monitoring continues normally.

SCD41 CO₂ Calibration

SCD41 CO₂ FORCE RECALIBRATION

Force recalibration adjusts the SCD41 CO₂ sensor against fresh outdoor air.

Ensure the device has been exposed to fresh outdoor air (400 ppm) for at least 3 minutes before calibrating.

The ESP32 performs the calibration. This dashboard only sends the Firebase command.
