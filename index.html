Indoor Air Quality Guide

Simple ways to understand and improve indoor air conditions

Ventilate regularly

Open windows or use mechanical ventilation when CO2 rises.

Reduce VOC sources

Limit smoke, strong fragrances, aerosols, and harsh cleaning products.

Control particles

Reduce dust, smoke, cooking emissions, and other sources when PM2.5 is elevated.
