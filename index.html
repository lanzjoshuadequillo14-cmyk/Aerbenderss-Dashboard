Indoor Air Quality Guide

Simple ways to keep your indoor air healthy

Ventilate regularly

Open windows or use mechanical ventilation when CO2 rises.

Reduce VOC sources

Limit smoke, strong fragrances, aerosols, and harsh cleaning products.

Control particles

Reduce dust and smoke, and use air filtration when PM AQI is elevated.
